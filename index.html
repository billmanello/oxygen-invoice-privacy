Privacy Policy — Oxygen Invoice App

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Oxygen Invoice App collects the following data from your Shopify store: order information (order number, line items, total amounts, taxes), customer billing information (VAT number, company name, tax office) only when provided voluntarily at checkout, and shop configuration settings (Oxygen API credentials stored encrypted).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely to generate invoices and receipts via the Oxygen ERP API and to submit the required data to the Greek tax authority (myDATA/ΑΑΔΕ) as required by Greek law.

3. Data Storage

Order and invoice data is stored in a secure database hosted on Railway.app. Oxygen API credentials are stored encrypted. We do not sell or share your data with third parties except as required for invoice generation (Oxygen ERP) and tax submission (myDATA).

4. Data Retention

Invoice logs are retained for 5 years as required by Greek tax law. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

5. GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at: invoice-support@picture2magnet.com

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions: invoice-support@picture2magnet.com
